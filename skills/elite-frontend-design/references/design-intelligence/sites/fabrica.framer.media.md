## Visual Design Identity

A dense and premium **dark editorial system**: computed body rgb(18,18,18) / **#121212**; high contrast white/light content and medium-gray captions; **Inter / Inter Placeholder** display and body; restrained imagery and graphic gradients instead of ornamental canvas. Typical display H1 **148px on desktop ->64px phone** (Studio, Blog, Contact, projects details, legal); homepage H1 is intentionally a smaller premise **22px desktop ->18px mobile**, while next-level signature heading H2 **144px desktop ->62px phone**. Projects archive uses large H2 144→62 with no observed H1: fix semantic heading hierarchy when implementing. Site does not contain a canvas on the sampled homepage or representative routes. Homepage has **64 image DOM elements, 2 video** desktop and phone, with page height **17,190px desktop, 19,812px mobile**; video DOM alone is not proof of autoplay, downloaded size or playback.

Stylistic mechanics: split case-preview reels, unusually large item-counter/label typography, close-cropped staff photo inserts, short marquee-like role descriptions, oversized stats, repeated "fabrica" typographic brand footer, image-led four service lanes, dark cards/photos/soft gradients and very long narrative scroll. Responsive strategy reflows headings and grid media rather than squeezing the desktop composition; image identity preserved and document has zero outer overflow at 390px.

## Transferable Design Methods

**Dark Agency Work-first Sequence** — clear no-fluff promise -> actual featured cases -> four concrete service families -> process and scope -> proof -> pricing -> contact. Project links provide evidence instead of generic bento.
**Editorial H1/H2 Role Separation** — small home premise H1 and massive 144px visual H2 can work, but each route must have clear semantic document H1; projects archive missing H1 is an accessibility/SEO QA issue.
**Queryable Portfolio Taxonomy** — native search and category select with genuine filtered result-set behavior; ensure no-match/reset/keyboard and count announcement before production.
**Cross-sell Scope and Marketing Addon** — home Pricing with per-project vs monthly affordances, clearly delineated design/build base and SEO/marketing addon, never imply live checkout or correct plan switch from labels alone.
**Project/Blog Full-Index Equality** — six case/ seven editorial URLs reach via their archives. A long-form article is a matched title/body/heading/metadata/author image unit, not merely CMS collection records.
**Low-friction Contact Boundary** — required Name/Email + transparent data policy creates an easy entry point but cannot substitute for project scoping; add message/service/budget intentionally where business needs justify it.
**Measured Motion Without Canvas** — Inter type, media/video and scroll hierarchy create premium experience without requiring WebGL/canvas; honor reduced motion and static poster, avoid page-load assumptions based on media nodes.
**Template Publisher and Proof Firewall** — separate studio-case claims/socials from template creator store, numeric achievement samples and incomplete CMS meta. Never reuse copyright-protected images, exact layout, text or claims verbatim.
