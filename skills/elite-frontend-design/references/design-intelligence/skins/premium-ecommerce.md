# Baseline Skin: Premium Ecommerce

## Use when
Use for high-quality product commerce where imagery, product details, trust, and conversion must coexist without feeling like a generic marketplace grid.

## Deterministic Design Scaffold
Let product photography/material evidence carry identity while transaction interaction remains familiar and conversion-safe.

## Token Scaffold
- warm/cool neutral page chosen to flatter product media;
- page / product-detail / transactional-control surface hierarchy;
- high-contrast text; border 8–12% neutral contrast;
- radius 6–14px, lower on editorial media than controls;
- container 1240–1400px; detail column 420–560px;
- product title 42–64px desktop, 34–46px mobile.

## Composition Grammar
- first fold exposes product, price/availability context, key choice/action, and high-quality media;
- gallery prioritizes inspection over decorative carousel chrome;
- separate story/craft chapters from transaction UI;
- delivery/returns/sizing/compatibility proof appears near the decision it de-risks.

## Skin Lock
Lock product-media treatment, transaction controls, type hierarchy, surface levels, spacing, and selection feedback.

## Controlled Mutation
May mutate editorial story sections, accent, gallery composition, material storytelling, and one signature product interaction.

## Responsive Contract
Keep product selection, price/availability, action, and gallery reachable; never sacrifice checkout-critical clarity for editorial composition.

## Anti-Template Guard
Avoid default marketplace card grids when the product deserves deeper inspection or editorial storytelling.

## QA Rubric
Verify product inspection, selection states, trust placement, conversion path, gallery accessibility, mobile purchase flow, and editorial/transaction separation.
