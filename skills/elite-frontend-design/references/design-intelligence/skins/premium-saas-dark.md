# Baseline Skin: Premium SaaS Dark

Powder audited evidence anchor: ../sites/powder.framer.website.md

This skin is the dark B2B/SaaS deterministic scaffold. Powder now provides an audited whole-site evidence anchor for the skin, but the skin is broader than Powder and must not copy its exact brand, mockups, copy, metrics, colors, or Framer-specific template chrome.

## Use when

Use when a B2B/SaaS product needs:
- a premium dark marketing shell;
- believable product/workspace proof;
- restrained motion;
- strong hierarchy across a long page;
- pricing, FAQ, content, legal/company, or conversion routes that need to remain visually coherent.

Especially useful for:
- AI agents and copilots;
- automation/workflow SaaS;
- observability/ops products;
- enterprise tools;
- technical products where a dark shell supports focus.

Prefer developer-infra.md when authentic code/API evidence should dominate.
Prefer enterprise-ai-platform-light.md when a light enterprise system is a better fit.

## Deterministic Design Scaffold

Use this as a coherent low-variance fallback, not as a Linear clone and not as a Powder clone.

The scaffold should remain convincing before bespoke illustration or complex motion is added.

## Token Scaffold

- page shell: near-black neutral;
- main surfaces: +3-7% luminance above shell;
- nested product surfaces: another +3-6% luminance where hierarchy needs it;
- primary text: 90-96% white or slightly warm off-white;
- muted text: 55-68% white equivalent;
- hairline border: 8-14% white equivalent;
- stronger separator: 18-28% white equivalent;
- one product-specific accent plus semantic state colors;
- display: clean sans, roughly 52-72px desktop / 34-42px mobile;
- body: 16-18px with comfortable line height;
- utility/data role: compact sans or mono, used selectively;
- radius family: 8 / 12 / 16 / 24px;
- pill radius: reserve 999px for compact actions/chips, not major containers;
- container: roughly 1120-1280px;
- section gap: roughly 104-152px desktop, 64-96px mobile;
- shadow: optional and weak; prefer luminance + border separation before glow.

Powder evidence shows a useful dark hierarchy built mostly from black/charcoal layering, translucent white rules, warm off-white text, and restrained muted accents rather than constant neon bloom.

## Composition Grammar

### Home

A reliable dark SaaS sequence:

1. one clear promise;
2. one authentic proof surface/signature product state;
3. ecosystem/context proof;
4. 2-4 deeper capability chapters;
5. operational or workflow evidence;
6. integrations / connected systems;
7. product-evolution proof such as changelog when real;
8. outcomes/metrics only when substantiated;
9. pricing or packaging;
10. FAQ/trust;
11. social proof;
12. content/resources;
13. final conversion.

Do not require every route/section above. Use only what the product can support truthfully.

### Product proof

Prefer:
- one recurring product information model;
- readable state changes;
- inputs + outputs;
- visible source/context when trust matters;
- actual artifact/action/result.

Avoid a gallery of unrelated decorative dashboards.

## Conversation-to-Action Proof

For agent/automation products, use the audited Powder lesson:

**intent / question -> contextual answer -> source / trust -> artifact or action -> next operational state**

Requirements:
- show what the user is trying to do;
- show context/provenance when the product relies on it;
- show what was produced or changed;
- make the next state legible;
- keep the same product model across sections;
- never fabricate tools, sources, integrations, actions, approvals, or autonomy.

A dark chat mockup is not sufficient proof by itself.

## Sticky Capability Stack

Optional desktop mechanism, not a skin requirement.

Use when multiple sequential capabilities benefit from one persistent product stage:

- keep the major proof shell spatially stable;
- each scroll step must materially change meaning or state;
- reserve enough vertical reading distance;
- avoid stacking more steps than the visitor can remember;
- release sticky behavior on small screens when viewport geometry becomes cramped;
- preserve the narrative order after release.

Powder's audited desktop uses large position: sticky product-proof stages and releases that geometry on mobile. Transfer the responsive logic, not the exact dimensions.

## Route-System Grammar

A premium dark site should remain coherent outside the landing page.

Useful route roles:
- about/company: quieter trust and team story;
- changelog/releases: product maturity;
- pricing: packaging without abandoning product material language;
- blog/resources: calmer editorial typography;
- legal/privacy: readable long-form dark mode without decorative noise;
- conversion: focused form, minimal distraction;
- 404/error: on-brand but clearly utility content.

Use Template Residue Quarantine when auditing or adapting public template routes.

## Template Residue Quarantine

When using a template as an evidence anchor:
- include utility/demo routes in audit coverage;
- classify them separately;
- do not let remix/editor/vendor chrome enter the brand system;
- do not promote isolated component/demo pages into the core composition;
- preserve useful utility behavior only when the new product needs it.

## Skin Lock

Lock:
- dark luminance hierarchy;
- typography roles;
- border/surface hierarchy;
- radius family;
- spacing rhythm;
- primary/secondary action treatment;
- one coherent proof information model;
- restrained motion;
- mobile release rules for desktop-only geometry.

## Controlled Mutation

May mutate:
- brand accent;
- warm vs cool neutral bias;
- hero alignment;
- font families;
- proof mechanism;
- card density;
- signature interaction;
- section order;
- product-specific media;
- whether sticky narrative is used at all.

Must mutate away from audited/template identity:
- Powder wordmark;
- exact Powder palette;
- exact product mockups;
- exact metrics/copy/testimonials;
- template remix/vendor chrome;
- exact sticky stage dimensions;
- exact article/changelog content.

## Responsive Contract

- preserve hierarchy and primary proof before desktop geometry;
- compress spacing before deleting identity;
- desktop grids collapse to one column when needed;
- release sticky proof narratives on mobile if viewport geometry makes them oppressive;
- keep the document itself free of unintended horizontal overflow;
- keep primary proof readable rather than shrinking it into illegibility;
- mobile navigation may reduce surface area but must preserve core conversion;
- pricing must remain comparable/readable;
- article/legal typography must remain comfortably scannable;
- sticky sub-navigation is allowed only if it leaves enough reading viewport.

## Motion Language

- motion should communicate state, progression, or continuity;
- avoid universal fade-up on every block;
- sticky narratives are optional and should earn their scroll cost;
- marquee motion should be decorative/social-proof support, never required to read content;
- accordion transitions stay fast;
- avoid global cursor/scroll gimmicks unless they serve product meaning;
- respect reduced-motion expectations when implementing new work.

## Anti-Template Guard

High-risk commodity bundle:

- near-black shell;
- tiny mono labels;
- translucent rounded cards;
- centered hero;
- purple/blue/rose glow;
- floating dashboard mockups;
- integration grid;
- pricing;
- FAQ;
- testimonial marquee;
- gradient CTA.

Any single trait can be appropriate. Using the entire bundle unchanged creates template convergence.

Require at least one structural differentiator in:
- proof mechanism;
- information model;
- typography role system;
- hero composition;
- interaction grammar;
- responsive transformation;
- content architecture.

Changing only accent color, icons, glow, or radius does not count.

## QA Rubric

Pass only if:
- type roles and spacing cadence are coherent;
- surface hierarchy works without relying on excessive glow;
- muted text/borders remain readable;
- the main proof belongs to the actual product;
- Conversation-to-Action Proof is truthful when used;
- sticky narrative steps each add meaning;
- mobile releases desktop-only sticky geometry cleanly;
- the outer document has no unintended horizontal overflow;
- pricing/content/legal/company routes still feel like the same system;
- template/vendor residue is quarantined;
- mono is a role, not a costume;
- the final design has a product-specific differentiator beyond "premium dark SaaS."
